🗑️ Account Deletion Request

How to request deletion of your Community Library account and data

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Open the Community Library app and go to Profile > Settings > Delete Account.
  2. Follow the on-screen instructions to confirm your request.
  3. If you cannot access the app, you can submit a request using the form at the bottom of this page.

What Happens When You Delete Your Account?

Data Type Deleted? Retention Period
Account Information (name, email, password) Yes Deleted within 30 days
Profile Information (profile picture, display name) Yes Deleted within 30 days
Book Data (books you added) Yes Deleted within 30 days
Community Memberships Yes Deleted within 30 days
Transaction Data (borrowing/lending history) Yes Retained for 3 years for dispute resolution
Usage Analytics (anonymized) No (anonymized and retained) Indefinitely

Note: Some data may be retained for legal or security reasons as required by law.

Who Can Request Deletion?

Only the account owner can request deletion. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has created an account, please contact us at theme.innov8@gmail.com.
